Open-plan living spaces extend to generous entertainment areas and a sunset-facing pool, creating a calm and private estate home designed for modern living.

Set within the secure Blue Hills Equestrian Estate, this ultra luxury modern mansion offers scale, privacy, and effortless indoor-outdoor living on a generous stand.

The residence features expansive open-plan living areas, a designer kitchen with scullery, five en-suite bedrooms, and multiple entertainment spaces including a private cinema, leisure level, and pool house overlooking a sunset-facing pool. Large glass openings connect the interiors to landscaped outdoor areas, making the home ideal for both everyday living and entertaining.

A well-considered balance of luxury and functionality, this home is designed for buyers seeking space, comfort, and secure estate living just minutes from key amenities.
