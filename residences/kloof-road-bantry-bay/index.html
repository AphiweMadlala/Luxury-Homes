Experience unmatched luxury at this five-story home atop Bantry Bay with stunning Atlantic Ocean views. This architectural masterpiece blends opulence and practicality, offering five en suite bedrooms with private balconies and breathtaking northern views. The master suite is a haven of indulgence with a spacious walk-in dressing area and a full bathroom. Enter into a grand hall featuring a penthouse office, chic guest bathroom, and easy access to all levels via a private lift.

The third level is designed for top-notch entertainment with open-concept living, dining, and kitchen areas leading to a rim-flow pool overlooking the ocean. The fourth level is an entertainer’s paradise with a state-of-the-art movie theatre, bar, pool table, and a tranquil jacuzzi haven. Additional luxurious features include a modern home cinema, open chef’s kitchen, walk-in fridges, and closets. The ground floor hosts a private wellness space with a fully equipped gym, restrooms, and laundry facilities.

The mansion’s elegant architectural lines, opulent interior furnishings, warm timber cladding, and floor-to-ceiling glass doors enhance the spacious living areas. Situated in one of Cape Town’s most sought-after neighborhoods, residents enjoy unparalleled security, world-class dining, pristine beaches, and vibrant nightlife. This remarkable mansion sets a new standard for luxurious living on the Atlantic Coast.
