Cantilever House, Higgovale

Discover an extraordinary sanctuary nestled among the trees in the prestigious suburb of Higgovale. This architecturally acclaimed luxury home offers a captivating blend of serenity and sophistication. Elevated above the surrounding landscape, it boasts two remarkable cantilevered concrete forms that stretch gracefully across an infinity pool, providing an awe-inspiring backdrop of the city skyline and the vast ocean beyond.

Immerse yourself in the seamless fusion of indoor and outdoor living as the expansive living area effortlessly flows onto a spacious deck, revealing a state-of-the-art open-plan kitchen equipped with the finest Miele appliances. Ascend to the upper floor, where the master suite awaits, a private retreat of opulence and elegance, complete with a panoramic terrace offering sweeping views of the city, the harbor, and the majestic peaks of Devil's Peak and Lion's Head.

The ground floor offers versatility, whether it's a third bedroom, a staff suite, or a personal gym. This exceptional residence is not only a haven of luxury but also embraces advanced security features, cutting-edge smart technology, and a sustainable solar power system, ensuring a truly elevated living experience that is both indulgent and environmentally conscious.
