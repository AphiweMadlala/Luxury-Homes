House, Benmore Gardens

This contemporary family home, designed by Arthur Quinton, boasts 4 en-suite bedrooms, 2 spacious living areas, a sleek kitchen, and a beautifully manicured garden with a sparkling solar-heated pool and gas braai area. Enjoy stunning views of the Benmore farm from the cul-de-sac location. Additional features include staff accommodation, double garage, and a security system with electric fencing and beams. Conveniently located near top schools, shopping centers, and entertainment, this home offers the perfect combination of privacy and accessibility.
