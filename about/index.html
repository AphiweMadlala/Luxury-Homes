About

Luxury Homes South Africa presents the country’s most exquisite homes, and credits the agents, architects and designers behind them.

It began on Instagram, where the collection has grown to more than 67 000 followers, and continues on YouTube, where full-length house tours have been published since 2020.

The collection covers homes on the market, recently completed houses, interiors and projects still in development, from the Atlantic Seaboard and the Winelands to Sandton, Pretoria and the KwaZulu-Natal north coast.

Luxury Homes South Africa is not an estate agency. Homes that are for sale are marketed by the agencies named on each listing, and enquiries about them go to those agents.
