# Business Model Audit: Luxury Homes South Africa

Research date: 2026-09-24. Primary source: https://www.instagram.com/luxuryhomes_southafrica/

Evidence labels: **VERIFIED** (stated by a primary source), **INFERRED** (strongly implied by several sources, not stated outright), **UNVERIFIED** (single secondary claim, or not confirmable).

## Conclusion

Luxury Homes South Africa is **(C) a luxury property media platform + (E) a property-tour content brand + (F) a property-marketing / feature service**. It is **not an estate agency** and does not hold listing mandates. Homes are presented on behalf of the agencies, architects, designers and developers who own or represent them.

It is not (A) an estate agency or (B) an individual agent brand, although its founder has worked as a property practitioner (see below). The architecture/design publication role (D) is real but secondary: most 2021–2022 posts are credited architecture features.

### Hamilton's Property Portfolio

- **VERIFIED (historic):** Thobela Cars' About page states Mbuyelo Rathidili previously worked with Hamilton's Property Portfolio. One 2022 post names @hamiltons_property_portfolio as the agency for a Bryanston listing.
- **UNVERIFIED (current):** Hamilton's agent page and news article URLs return 404. No current relationship could be confirmed.
- **Decision:** the website does **not** state or imply any relationship between the platform and Hamilton's. The one Bryanston archive listing credits Hamilton's as that listing's agency only.

### Cacoon Group

- **VERIFIED:** every contact email in captions uses @cacoongroup.com (sales@, marketing@, design@, rentals@, info@, media@, Mbuyelo@); the YouTube channel links the Cacoon Group Facebook page.
- **INFERRED:** Cacoon Group is the operating company behind the platform. It is not described anywhere as an estate agency.
- **Decision:** the site names Cacoon Group only in the footer as operator of the contact addresses; no corporate history is invented.

### Thobela Cars
