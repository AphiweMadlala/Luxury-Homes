# Brand Audit

Based on the 200 most recent posts (1,267 media items reviewed as contact sheets), the profile image, and 9 YouTube posters and descriptions.

## Existing brand elements

| Element | What the feed shows |
|---|---|
| Name | "Luxury Homes South Africa" (channel, hashtags); Instagram display name "Luxury Real Estate & Design". |
| Mark | Profile image: two tall slabs, black and mid-grey, with gold serif capitals "L" and "H". No other logo file in the feed. |
| Tagline | "A Collection of the Most Exquisite Homes represented by the Who's Who in Real Estate, Architecture, and Design." |
| Video graphics | YouTube posters: widely spaced classical serif capitals in white over the photograph (e.g. "RIVERS EDGE", "BUSH OUTLOOK", "R39 990 000"), small logo mark bottom-left, practice name as a small caption. |
| Caption voice | Aspirational, confident, concise. Price/spec blocks with emoji bullets (💰 🛏 🛁 🚗 📏 📐 📍). Credits in the form "Designed by @practice". Sign-off: "Stay inspired and follow @Luxuryhomes_southafrica for more of South Africa's Finest Homes." |
| Content rhythm | 2021: near-daily architecture features and listings. 2022: listings and credited features. 2023 onward: few, larger pieces: long-form tours (19–42 min) and premium listings (R31m–R220m). |
| Photography | Professional architectural photography supplied by agencies and practices: wide-angle, high saturation, deep-blue skies, turquoise pools, lush lawns, frequent dusk shots with warm interior glow. Reels are handheld gimbal walk-throughs. |
| Architecture | Contemporary South African residential: flat-roof volumes, cantilevered slabs, double-volume glazing, timber cladding and screens, natural stone walls, charcoal aluminium frames, rim-flow pools; some pitched-roof "modern farmhouse" and Cape vernacular reinterpretations. |
| Interiors | Pale stone and porcelain floors, warm timber ceilings, sculptural lighting, statement art; occasional maximalist (Black Coffee penthouse). |
| Geography | Sandton and northern Johannesburg estates, Pretoria East, Atlantic Seaboard, Constantia and Winelands, Garden Route, KZN north coast. |
| Adjacent content | Supercars and motivational quotes (2021), automotive sponsor (Thobela Cars). |

## Recurring materials (for colour derivation)

1. Board-formed concrete and grey limestone (facades, floors): cool, slightly green-grey.
2. Charcoal powder-coated aluminium (every window frame, screens).
3. Pool water: turquoise to deep teal, present in roughly half of all cover images.
4. Timber: warm, but always as an accent within grey/white volumes.
5. Kikuyu lawn green and indigenous planting.

## New website interpretation

| Existing | Website |
|---|---|
| Black/grey slabs + gold letters | Same two-slab structure, rendered in the site's ink and grey with ground-coloured letters. **Interim**: the client's vector mark should replace it. Gold is not carried into the UI. |
| Classical serif caps on posters | Not reused as display type (it would read as generic luxury). The serif voice survives as Newsreader for ledes and stories; headings use Schibsted Grotesk. |
| Emoji spec blocks | Replaced by a particulars register (Accommodation / Land and costs / Location) and a one-line spec summary. |
| "Designed by @practice" credits | Promoted to a first-class component: credits sit beside every house title and link to an architect index. |
| Concrete, aluminium, pool water | Palette: Concrete `#EEF0EF`, Aluminium `#15191B`, Water `#0F6B6E`. |
| Tours on YouTube | Films section; click-to-load player; stills from the brand's own reels. |
| Caption voice | Kept factual. Ledes paraphrase captions; story paragraphs are the brand's own caption text with emoji and dashes removed. |
| Supercars, quotes, rentals | Left out. |

## Titles

Official names are used wherever the source gives one (House VG5, Oban House, Rivers Edge, Sun Valley Manor, Bush Outlook, Can Balearia, The Boundary House). Elsewhere titles are descriptive and derived from the caption ("House, Waterkloof Ridge", "Cantilever House, Higgovale"). They are editorial labels, not official names, and should be reviewed by the client.
