Feature a home

Luxury Homes South Africa features listings, projects and designs from agents, architects, interior designers and developers across South Africa.

Features appear on Instagram, and selected homes are filmed as full-length tours for YouTube. Every feature credits the people behind the home.

Who we feature

  • Estate agentsListings for sale, credited to the agent and agency
  • ArchitectsCompleted houses, credited to the practice
  • Interior designersInteriors, credited to the studio
  • DevelopersProjects and estates, including homes still in development

What to send

  • The homeThe property or project name, and where it is
  • The creditsWho designed it: architect, interior designer, developer
  • For listingsThe listing agent, agency and asking price, with a link to the listing
  • The imageryPhotography, and video if you have it, with the photographer’s name
