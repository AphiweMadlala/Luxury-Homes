Houses / Architecture

Glass House, Serengeti

Serengeti Estates, Kempton Park

Architecture
Mtwa Architects

Classic Elegance, at first glance this home is a home that comes style and a strong sense of masculinity. The design and layout of the house is one of transparency explains Paul Mtwa of Mtwa Architects.

Expansive glazing forms a soft shell around the house, bringing the exterior in and interior out. It’s east west axis invites the northern sunlight into all the living and private spaces. Once inside, the living and entertainment spaces lead into one another without any barriers, and this creates the foundation for a contemporary experience.

First featured August 2021. Source: Instagram post.
